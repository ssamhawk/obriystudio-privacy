Orbi Support

Answers for the current Android release.

Need help? Email support@obriystudio.app. Include your device model, Android version, Orbi version, and a short description of what happened. Do not send passwords or payment-card information.

Where is my progress?

Orbi currently saves progress on the device. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the Game may remove it. Cloud save is not active in version 1.0.0.

Does Orbi need internet?

Core gameplay is designed to work without a constant connection. Internet access may be needed for analytics delivery, crash reports, support links, store services, and required update checks.

Why is an update required?

A required update may be used for compatibility, stability, security, or store compliance. If the update screen appears, install the latest public Google Play version before continuing.

How do I delete local data?

Use Android Settings → Apps → Orbi → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall Orbi. This removes local saves and settings from that device.

Are ads or purchases active?

No. Version 1.0.0 does not include active advertising or real-money purchases. Any future release that adds them will update the store disclosures and Privacy Policy first.

How do I report a bug?

Describe what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened. A screenshot or short screen recording can help, but avoid including private information.

Privacy Requests

For privacy questions or rights requests, use the same support address with the subject “Privacy Request”. See the Privacy Policy for available choices and limitations caused by the absence of named accounts.
