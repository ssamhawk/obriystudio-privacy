Orbi Support

Android | Updated November 3, 2026.

Contact

Email support@obriystudio.app.

Include the app version, Android version, device model, mode, approximate time and reproduction steps. A screenshot or recording can help. Do not send passwords, payment details, advertising IDs or sensitive information.

Local Progress And Deletion

On Android, use Settings, Apps, Orbi, Storage, Clear storage (names may vary). This removes local progress and settings. Platform backups or restored app data can restore saves. Manage backups separately in device or account settings.

This release has no Orbi accounts, cloud saves or online ranking. Support cannot promise recovery of deleted local progress. Deleting the app does not delete previously collected provider records or support correspondence. For privacy access or deletion requests, email the contact above; applicable rights and limits are explained in the Privacy Policy.

Privacy Requests

To request access, correction or deletion of data, email support@obriystudio.app with the subject "Orbi privacy request" and describe what you want to request. No Orbi account or login is needed. Include the app version and approximate dates if known. We may ask for limited information needed to locate relevant records or verify the request; never send passwords or payment details. Requests are assessed under applicable law. Some records may need to be retained for legal or security reasons, and anonymous records may not be identifiable. Provider-specific privacy links are in the Privacy Policy.

Purchases And Rewarded Ads

Android supports optional rewarded ads for the displayed in-game benefit. There are no real-money purchases, subscriptions, banners, forced interstitials or app-open ads. Ad availability is not guaranteed. A failed or early-closed ad without confirmed completion does not earn a reward. Closing after confirmed completion does not itself cancel a reward. For a missing reward, send the placement and approximate time, not device identifiers. Ordinary play and rewards must not require tracking permission.

Reminders And Privacy Choices

Return reminders are local notifications, not remote pushes. Disable them in game settings or device notification settings. Where Analytics is enabled, reminder opens can be recorded. Gameplay Analytics can be disabled in Settings. Advertising privacy options are available when required by the SDK; device advertising controls are separate choices. The Analytics switch does not change your advertising privacy choice.

Configuration Updates

Automatic downloads are limited to HTTPS versioned balance and minimum-build JSON, with no code or asset OTA downloads. They are not cloud saves and can use network data and local cache storage.
