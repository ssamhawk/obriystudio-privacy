Orbi Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: August 17, 2026

Summary: Orbi works without a game account. Progress and settings stay on the device. Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics provide anonymous usage metrics and crash diagnostics. Advertising ID collection and ad personalization are disabled.

Scope

This policy explains how OBRIY STUDIO ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in the Android game Orbi. It applies to version 1.0.0 and other versions that link to this policy.

Information Stored On The Device

Orbi may store the following information locally to preserve gameplay between sessions:

  • profile name entered by the player;
  • progress, unlocked arenas, contracts, rank, rewards, and run history;
  • in-game gold and crystal balances;
  • language, music, sound effects, and haptic settings;
  • local onboarding and tutorial state.

The profile name and exact local save data are not included in Firebase events.

Information We Collect Or Share

Firebase Analytics may process a random app-instance identifier, app lifecycle and session information, gameplay events such as run starts and ends, run duration, level progression and upgrade choices, app version, language, device model, operating-system information, and a coarse region derived by Google from a masked IP address.

Firebase Crashlytics may process installation identifiers, crash and non-fatal error traces, diagnostic state, app version, device model, and operating-system information. Orbi does not set a Firebase user ID, send the profile name, or attach exact local save data. Advertising ID collection, ad personalization, advertising SDKs, accounts, cloud saves, and payment SDKs are not enabled.

Google Play and the device manufacturer may independently process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

Use, Retention, And Deletion

Local data provides game saves and continuity. Firebase data is used to measure audience size, sessions, retention, gameplay funnels and feature use, and to diagnose stability problems. It is not used for behavioral advertising or cross-app tracking.

Local data remains until the player clears app storage or uninstalls Orbi. Firebase retains analytics and diagnostics according to our Firebase and Google Analytics retention settings. Orbi has no user account and does not set a Firebase user ID, so we generally cannot associate an installation record with a named person.

Security

Firebase data is encrypted in transit. We avoid Advertising ID and billing permissions and limit custom events to gameplay and diagnostics. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.

Children

The game is not directed to children below the applicable digital-consent age. It has no chat, accounts, user-generated content, behavioral advertising, or online social features. Advertising identifiers and ad personalization are disabled.

International Transfers

Google may process Firebase data on servers outside the player's country under Google's terms and data-protection arrangements.

Third-Party Services

Orbi uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. See the Google Privacy Policy and Firebase privacy information.

Future Features

Before a release adds advertising, purchases, cloud saves, accounts, or other online services, we will update this policy, Google Play Data Safety answers, the SDK inventory, and required controls.

Changes

We may update this policy for product, legal, or technical changes. The revised policy will show a new last-updated date.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, email support@obriystudio.app.
