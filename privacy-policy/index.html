Orbi Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: November 3, 2026.

This policy describes Orbi for Android, provided under the OBRIY STUDIO name. Privacy contact: support@obriystudio.app.

Platform Scope

Orbi supports optional Appodeal rewarded ads and uses one Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) privacy flow, integrated with Appodeal, for the configured advertising and Analytics choices. There is no separate Firebase Analytics permission screen. Where that flow reports consent does not apply to Analytics, ordinary gameplay Analytics may operate under the regional default, subject to the Settings opt-out. This is not recorded as a user consent grant and does not grant advertising consent. Android diagnostics have a separate, legacy-permission boundary described below. You can turn off gameplay Analytics in Settings without losing progress or access to the game. This release offers no Orbi accounts, cloud saves, online leaderboards or real-money purchases.

Local Data And Reminders

Orbi stores the player-entered profile name, progress, run history, virtual balances, unlocks, preferences and tutorial state on the device. Optional return reminders are scheduled locally with operating-system permission; their payload can include reminder kind and mission ID. There is no remote push service for these reminders. Disable them in the game or device notification settings. Where Analytics is enabled, reminder-open and subsequent gameplay events can be sent to Firebase; local scheduling does not mean all reminder activity is private.

Data Sent To Providers

When enabled by an Analytics consent choice or the regional default described above, Firebase Analytics on Android processes app-instance identifiers, sessions, gameplay/progression, reward/ad outcomes, virtual-economy and performance events, app/device/OS details and coarse region derived from IP information. Advertising storage, ad-user-data and personalization signals are separate CMP decisions; the app does not force a blanket denial of those signals after the CMP resolves. Allowing gameplay Analytics, including through the regional default, does not automatically allow advertising uses or personalized ads.

Android Crashlytics diagnostics can resume only where a retained earlier custom permission explicitly covered diagnostics, the current CMP explicitly grants Analytics storage and the Settings opt-out is not active. A new CMP Analytics grant, a new Settings enable action or the regional default does not create diagnostics permission. Turning gameplay Analytics off also disables diagnostics and clears that retained diagnostics permission; turning Analytics back on does not renew it. Permitted diagnostics process installation identifiers, crash/error traces, app state and device metadata; supporting Firebase services can process installation and session identifiers. Orbi does not intentionally attach profile names or complete saves to telemetry. Pseudonymous identifiers are not anonymous data.

When enabled, Appodeal, mediated ad networks and attribution services can process advertising/device identifiers (including Android Advertising ID), IP/network information, device and app details, ad impressions/clicks, performance, attribution and fraud signals. Partners may infer approximate location from IP; Orbi does not need GPS permission for gameplay or rewarded ads. Android dependencies include AdMob, BidMachine, Bidon, Meta Audience Network, Unity Ads, AppsFlyer and Meta Analytics. Partners and collection depend on the platform, SDKs, consent and service configuration. Advertising services may initialize or request/cache ads before an ad is watched.

Rewarded ads are optional offers for the displayed in-game benefit. The game uses the SDK's completion result to decide a reward, and can record placement, outcome and impression revenue where telemetry is enabled. No banner, forced interstitial or app-open formats are intended for this release.

Automatic configuration downloads are limited to versioned JSON balance and minimum-build information over HTTPS. Initially there are no code or asset OTA downloads, accounts or cloud saves. When enabled, the host receives IP address and normal request metadata (such as requested version and request time) and may retain access logs. Local cached data supports game delivery. The configured host is GitHub Pages at orbi.obriystudio.app. GitHub handles hosting request data under its own privacy policy. Google Play update checks can also exchange device and app-version metadata with Google Play. App stores and operating-system backups follow their own policies.

If you contact support, we receive your email address and what you choose to send to resolve the request. Do not send sensitive information or advertising IDs.

Purposes, Choices And Rights

Local saves and content delivery provide the game; diagnostics maintain stability; Analytics measures usage and balancing; advertising services deliver, measure and secure ads and attribute campaigns. Support correspondence helps resolve requests. Depending on processing and applicable law, the legal basis may be providing the requested service, legitimate interests in stability/security, legal duties, or consent. Accepting Terms or watching an ad is not consent to tracking. The regional Analytics default is not recorded as consent and does not authorize advertising personalization. Applicable consent and privacy requirements vary by region.

The shared UMP flow can collect separate Analytics and advertising choices. Analytics remains off until a fresh CMP result supports an explicit Analytics grant or the regional default. Rejection, missing or invalid results, and an unconfigured Analytics purpose do not enable it. A saved Settings opt-out overrides either route; switching Analytics on in Settings cannot override a CMP rejection. Availability of ads is not treated as Analytics consent.

Advertising privacy options are available when required by the SDK; you can also use device advertising controls. The gameplay Analytics switch does not replace advertising privacy options or change your advertising choices. Refusing tracking must not prevent ordinary play or be made a condition of receiving a reward.

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, object to processing, or withdraw consent without affecting prior lawful processing. Contact support@obriystudio.app. You may complain to your local data-protection supervisory authority. We may need limited information to locate a record or verify a request; lack of an Orbi account does not remove these rights.

Retention, Transfers And Security

Clearing app storage or deleting the app removes local saves subject to platform backup/restore behavior. Cached data can be downloaded again. Uninstalling does not delete provider records, support email or backups, or stop processing of already collected data. We retain support correspondence while needed to resolve the request, handle follow-up or meet legal duties. Provider retention depends on configured periods, purposes and legal duties; the provider policies linked below explain their practices. Contact us about deletion requests.

Providers may process data outside your country. Applicable transfer safeguards and provider agreements must cover those transfers. HTTPS/TLS protects network transport where configured; no system is completely secure.

Children And Changes

The game is intended for teenagers and adults, with a target audience of 13 and over, not children under 13. Local age and parental-permission requirements may differ. A content rating is not consent to data processing. There is no chat or user-generated public content in this release. Contact us if a child's information was collected. Material changes will be reflected in an updated policy before changed processing ships. Contact: support@obriystudio.app.
