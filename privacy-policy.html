Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: August 22, 2026

Plain-language summary: Orbi works without a game account. Progress and settings stay on the device. Google Firebase provides usage metrics and crash diagnostics. Supported versions may offer optional rewarded ads through Appodeal. Orbi does not show forced interstitial or banner ads and does not currently process in-app payments.

1. Who This Policy Covers

This Privacy Policy explains how OBRIY STUDIO ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in the Android game Orbi (the "Game"), the Orbi website, and related support communications. It applies to version 1.0.0 and later versions that link to this policy.

2. Information Stored On Your Device

Orbi stores information locally so gameplay can continue between sessions. This may include:

  • the profile name entered by the player;
  • progress, unlocked content, contracts, Space Rank, rewards, and run history;
  • in-game gold and crystal balances;
  • language, music, sound effects, and haptic settings;
  • onboarding and tutorial state.

The profile name and exact local save data are not included in our Firebase events. Local progress remains on the device until app storage is cleared or the Game is uninstalled.

3. Information Processed By Current Services

Firebase Analytics

Firebase Analytics may process a random app-instance identifier, app lifecycle and session information, gameplay events such as run starts and ends, run duration, progression and upgrade choices, app version, language, device model, operating-system information, and a coarse region derived by Google from a masked IP address.

Firebase Crashlytics

Firebase Crashlytics may process installation identifiers, crash and non-fatal error traces, diagnostic state, app version, device model, and operating-system information. We do not set a Firebase user ID, send the player's profile name, or attach exact local save data to crash reports.

Google Play Update Checks

When Orbi checks whether a required update is available, Google Play may process device metadata, the installed app version, and installed modules or asset packs. This processing is performed by Google under its own terms.

Optional Rewarded Ads

When rewarded ads are enabled, Appodeal and its advertising, mediation, and measurement partners may process the Advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, device and operating-system information, app version, ad availability and interactions, diagnostics, and fraud-prevention signals. This information is used to request, deliver, limit, secure, measure, and attribute optional ads.

The Appodeal package used by Orbi includes AppsFlyer and Meta Analytics for campaign attribution and selected ad-demand providers. The provider for a particular impression may vary by region, device, inventory, and Appodeal configuration. A gameplay reward is granted only after the ad SDK confirms completion.

Support Messages

If you contact us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to provide. We use it only to respond, investigate the reported issue, protect the Game, and maintain a reasonable support record.

4. Why We Process Information

Local data is used to provide saves, progression, preferences, and gameplay continuity. Firebase information is used to measure audience size, sessions, retention, gameplay funnels and feature use, and to identify and fix stability problems. Advertising data is used to provide optional ads, confirm rewards, measure performance, prevent fraud, and attribute campaigns. Support information is used to respond to requests and diagnose issues.

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, processing is based on performance of the service requested by the player, our legitimate interests in operating and improving the Game, compliance with law, or consent where specifically requested.

5. Sharing And Service Providers

We do not sell personal information for money. Service providers include Google services for analytics, diagnostics, distribution and updates, plus Appodeal, AppsFlyer, Meta Analytics, and selected advertising providers when rewarded ads are enabled. They may process information as service providers or independent controllers under their own terms.

Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect players or the public, investigate fraud or security issues, or complete a business reorganization subject to appropriate safeguards.

6. Retention And Deletion

Local data remains until the player clears Orbi's app storage or uninstalls the Game. Firebase retains analytics and diagnostic data according to the retention controls configured for our Firebase and Google Analytics property. Uninstalling stops new collection.

Orbi has no user account and does not set a Firebase user ID, so we generally cannot connect an analytics installation record to a named person. Support correspondence is retained only as long as reasonably necessary to resolve the request, maintain records, or comply with law.

7. Your Choices And Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or objection, and to complain to a data-protection authority. Email support@obriystudio.app to submit a request. We may ask for information needed to verify and process it.

You can delete local game data by clearing app storage or uninstalling Orbi. Because the current Game has no account, cloud save, or named analytics profile, some requests may not be technically linkable to a specific installation.

Appodeal includes a consent solution based on Google User Messaging Platform. A consent form is shown when required by the player's region. Applicable users can revisit advertising and privacy choices from the Game's Settings screen. Device-level controls may also allow the Advertising ID to be reset or deleted.

8. Children

The Game is not directed to children below the applicable digital-consent age. It has no chat, account registration, user-generated content, or online social features, and is configured as not child-directed. Parents or guardians may contact us with privacy questions.

9. Security And International Processing

Firebase and advertising SDK data is encrypted in transit. We limit custom analytics events to gameplay, ad-result, and diagnostic events. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Google, Appodeal, and their partners may process information on servers outside the player's country under their data-protection terms and transfer mechanisms.

10. Advertising And Future Purchases

Supported versions may contain user-initiated rewarded ads. They do not contain banner ads, forced interstitial ads, or a No Ads purchase. Availability, frequency, and rewards may change as the Game is balanced.

Real-money purchases are not currently enabled. If purchases are enabled later, Google Play will process payment credentials. We may receive transaction status, product identifier, price and currency, but not full card details. Before release, we will update this policy and Google Play disclosures as required.

11. Website And Cookies

The Orbi website currently uses no analytics, advertising, or optional cookies. See our Cookies Policy for details. GitHub Pages may process technical request logs under GitHub's privacy terms to host and secure the site.

13. Changes And Contact

We may update this policy for product, legal, or technical changes. The current version and last-updated date will remain available on this page.

For privacy or support questions, email support@obriystudio.app.

Release note: Purchases, accounts, cloud saves, new ad formats, or new providers must not be enabled in a public build until this policy and the relevant store disclosures have been reviewed and updated.
